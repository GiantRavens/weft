# Review the insecure skaldic stanzas of Ragnars saga ch. 19 (lasi, mollu, sæki-tík) and 9.56-63 (móður föður) against Finnur Jónsson's Skjaldedigtning

## Notes

- 2026-10-22T10:55:12Z: Done 2026-10-22 against Finnur Jónsson, Skjaldedigtning B II (1915) pp. 253, 259-260 (archive.org dennorskislandsk04finn), and Egilsson, Lexicon Poeticum (1860). lasi/mollu: Finnur prints both phrases in quotes and leaves them untranslated ('nogle ord uforstål.'); English now marks them unintelligible, gap reworded. 9.56-63: Finnur supports 'like his mother, called his father's son' (no móður föður reading); takes Óðins ættar with the boy; English and snake-eye note updated, lætr/lét difference noted. 19.31-47: sæki-tík = wolf, at sverðs leiki = battle, harðmeldr Hveðnu = gold per Finnur, doubtful per Egilsson ([?]); roðna re-annotated as pp. of rjóða agreeing with svíra.
